🔹 Module‑level Evaluation Strategies
FeedForward

Measure reconstruction error or contribution to overall loss.

Ablation: remove or replace with linear layer and compare accuracy.

ConformerConvModule

Profile receptive field and local feature extraction.

Compare validation loss with and without this block.

RelativePositionEncoding

Evaluate attention alignment quality.

Visualize attention maps with vs. without relative encoding.

MultiHeadAttention

Inspect attention weights for interpretability.

Measure perplexity or alignment accuracy when heads are pruned.

ConformerBlock

Track gradient norms and contribution to convergence.

Ablation: replace with Transformer block and compare metrics.

ConformerEncoder

Evaluate encoder embeddings with auxiliary tasks (e.g., phoneme classification).

Use t‑SNE/UMAP to visualize clustering of encoded features.

LSTMDecoderBlock

Measure sequence prediction accuracy (cross‑entropy loss).

Compare BLEU or CER scores when swapping with Transformer decoder.

PhonemeDecoder

Track phoneme prediction accuracy directly.

Confusion matrix of predicted vs. target phonemes.

DurationPredictor

Evaluate mean absolute error (MAE) between predicted and ground‑truth durations.

Correlate predicted durations with alignment quality.

Upsampler

Measure reconstruction fidelity (MSE, PSNR) of upsampled features.

Compare synthesis quality with vs. without upsampling.

Synthesizer

Evaluate final audio quality with MOS (Mean Opinion Score).

Objective metrics: STOI, PESQ, or mel‑spectrogram distance.

🔹 Practical Tools in PyTorch
Hooks (register_forward_hook) → capture intermediate activations and compute module‑specific metrics.

Torch profiler → measure runtime and memory per module.

Auxiliary losses → attach small heads (e.g., classifiers) to intermediate outputs to quantify usefulness.

Ablation studies → disable or replace modules and measure performance drop.

🔹 Takeaway
You can measure each module’s performance by combining ablation studies, intermediate evaluations, profiling, and auxiliary losses. This gives you both quantitative metrics (loss, accuracy, MAE, MOS) and qualitative insights (attention maps, embeddings visualization).
