“The U2UT model configured with a GRU‑based phoneme decoder and GRU‑based synthesizer achieved validation accuracy of 96% by epoch 9 on the Yoruba–English dataset (1000/250 split). This performance surpasses earlier baselines, demonstrating the synergy between GRU’s efficient gating mechanism and the conformer encoder’s contextual modeling. The tuned attention configuration (MHSA = 4, cross‑attention = 2) further stabilized convergence, enabling rapid generalization. Prior research supports these findings, showing that GRUs often outperform LSTMs in low‑resource speech recognition due to their parameter efficiency and faster convergence (Chung et al., 2014; Khandelwal, Lecouteux, & Besacier, 2016; IEEE, 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Cornide‑Reyes et al., 2025; Chen, 2026).”
